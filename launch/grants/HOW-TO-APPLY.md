## 1. Aleo: Developer Grants (apply first, best fit)
- **Program page:** https://aleo.org/grants/
- **What you submit:**
  - a proposal doc in their template, from `aleo-scale-proposal.md`
  - a short form, answered in `aleo-form-answers.md`
- **Steps:**
  1. Copy the template: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1u_qGdlCVovocr91G78bw59poIMhvi9HU8DVqhpN0cy0
  2. Paste the sections from `aleo-scale-proposal.md`, then export as PDF.
     (Ready-made: `papilio-aleo-proposal.pdf` in this folder, same sections.)
  3. Submit the form: https://form.asana.com/?k=sCm0WT9M-V_fHl8AA48czQ&d=1199198487809755
- **Smaller tier:** the form asks whether you already have a **START** grant, which is a smaller first tier. If you'd rather start there, email **grants@aleo.org**:
  > "We're a student team with a live Aleo testnet dapp (Papilio, ZeFi). Should
  > we apply for a START grant first, and how?"
- **Size and payment:** up to $100K, paid per milestone.
- **Timeline:** first feedback is promised within ~1 week, then a technical review.
- **KYC/AML:** required for the whole team, but only if the grant goes ahead.
- **Category:** pick **ZeFi**.
- **The question they'll ask:** "how is this different from Shield Swap?" The proposal already answers it: Papilio routes private intent into *Ethereum* liquidity.

## 2. CoW DAO Grants Program
- **Where:** a forum post in https://forum.cow.fi/c/cow-grants-program/10
- **Steps:**
  1. Post the **scope check** from `cow-grant-application.md` and wait for replies (~1–2 weeks).
  2. Post the **full application**, adjusted to the committee's feedback.
  3. When the committee agrees, put it on Snapshot (https://snapshot.box/#/s:cowgrants.eth). This needs a tiny amount of xDAI on Gnosis to post. The vote lasts 3 days and needs 3 of 4 committee members.
- **Size:** recent grants are about $5–15K, paid in xDAI and/or COW per milestone.
- **Eligibility:** no US persons. No legal entity needed. An MIT license is fine.
- **What decides it:** a mainnet path plus a volume plan. A recent application was rejected for being off-CoW, so stress that Papilio settles on CoW.
